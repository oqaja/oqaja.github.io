Privacy Policy

Threads Affiliate · Last updated: 12 October 2026

Overview

Threads Affiliate is a private web app operated by a single individual, Oky Nurdianto ("the owner"), to manage the owner's own social media content. It is not a public service and is used by the owner only. This policy explains what Google data the app accesses and how it is handled.

Data accessed

When the owner authorizes the app with Google OAuth, the app requests only the https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file scope. This lets the app create and manage files in the owner's own Google Drive that the app itself created or opened. The app cannot see or modify any other file in the owner's Drive. The app does not collect personal data about any other person.

How data is used

The access is used only to upload image files for the owner's own content into the owner's own Google Drive. The data is not used for advertising, profiling or any other purpose.

Storage and security

The OAuth refresh token is stored as an encrypted environment variable in the owner's private hosting account and is used only by the app. Uploaded files are stored only in the owner's own Google Drive. No Google data is stored on any other server operated by the app.

Sharing

The app does not sell, rent or share Google user data with any third party. The use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access and deletion

The owner can revoke the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Revoking access immediately stops the app from reading or changing anything in the owner's Google account. Uploaded files can be deleted by the owner at any time from Google Drive.
