Threads Affiliate

A personal tool by Oky Nurdianto for managing his own social media content.

What it does

Threads Affiliate is a private web app used by its owner to manage a personal content schedule. When the owner connects Google Drive with Google OAuth, the app can upload image files that belong to the owner's own content (for example, pictures attached to scheduled posts) into the owner's own Google Drive.

Who can use it

Only the owner. The app is not offered to the public and has no sign-up. It does not access any Google account other than the owner's own.

Google access

The app requests only the drive.file permission, which limits it to files the app itself creates or opens. It cannot see the rest of the owner's Drive.
